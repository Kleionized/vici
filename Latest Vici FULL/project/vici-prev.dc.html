LESSON II
Riding the wave
An urge is not a command. It is a wave of sensation that rises, peaks in about ninety seconds, and falls — if you don't feed it.

Fighting the wave head-on exhausts you. Watching it pass builds the muscle that wins the month, not just the minute.
“You have power over your mind — not outside events.”
MARCUS AURELIUS
Tonight, when the wave rises, open the SOS drill and ride it out once. That's the whole assignment.
