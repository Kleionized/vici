Fifteen minutes, no switching
Choose one thing with a fixed end
one article, one chapter, one tutorial, one document, one piece of music, or another single item.
Close every unrelated tab or app. Turn off autoplay if it applies.
Put a sheet of paper beside you. Every time you want to switch tabs, check another app, search for something new, or skip ahead for novelty, make one tally mark and stay with the original item.
At the end, write the number of tally marks. Do not aim for zero; the count only shows how often you wanted to switch.

Now

Escape

Next time
Write what you could do instead, such as leaving an unsafe setting, grounding yourself in the room, calling a trusted person, writing down what is happening, or contacting a professional.
Prepare for that situation today. Save the contact, book the appointment, write down grounding steps, or tell the trusted person you may reach out. If you do not know of a trauma link, leave it there; do not invent one.
