What’s underneath it?
The urge is rarely the whole story. Name the feeling under it and it loses most of its grip.
Hungry
Low fuel reads as craving. Eat first.
Angry
Heat looking for the nearest exit.
Lonely
Reaching for any kind of contact.
Tired
Defenses are thinnest when you’re drained.
Bored
An empty minute asking to be filled.
Stressed
Pressure hunting a release valve.
H·A·L·T — the four states that fake an urge best.
