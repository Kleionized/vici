4 of 13 · What amor fati asks

What amor fati asks
Amor fati means “love of fate.” You do not have to pretend a relapse, breakup, or lost year was good. The practical demand is harder and more useful: stop spending the present trying to renegotiate a past that cannot change, and use what happened to decide what you do now.
5 of 13 · Keep the lesson, drop the al…
9:41

Keep the lesson, drop the alternate timeline
You can wish you had acted differently. You can apologise, repair what can still be repaired, learn from what happened, and choose what you will do differently the next time the same situation appears. What you cannot do is live inside an imagined version of the past where the mistake never happened. That version cannot change anything now; you can still decide what you do today.
