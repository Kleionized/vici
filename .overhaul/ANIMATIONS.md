# VICI: animation ideas

Ideas only. Nothing in this file is built. Each idea has a number so you can pick by number.

**Kind** says how big the change is:

- **New page**: a full screen that doesn't exist yet.
- **Existing**: a screen that exists today and stands still. The idea adds motion to it.
- **In place**: a small motion inside a screen you are already on. No new screen.

## Rules for all of them

- 2 to 3 seconds. A tap skips to the end.
- Black and cream only. No confetti, no sound.
- Show it right after the action that earned it, not at the next launch.
- One full page per launch at most. If two are due, the more important one shows and the other waits.
- Never in front of the SOS button.
- Each milestone page shows once per account.
- With Reduce Motion on, show the last frame with no movement.
- Things you can lose (a streak) get simple marks: circles and a number. Things you keep (the laurel, medallions) get
  the richer art. No page ever shows something being taken away.

## What moves today

Very little. Buttons press in, sheets slide up, switches slide, the spinner turns, the time wheel scrolls, the SOS
breathing disc grows and shrinks, and the urge ring counts down. Every other screen stands still.

## The streak rule

Decided. A day counts when it has a real check-in and no slip. A slip or a missed day resets the streak to 0. Today
doesn't break the run until the day is over. Your longest run is kept as "Best" (ideas 6 and 7).

## Left out on purpose

- **Confetti, fireworks and sound.** They don't fit the look or the voice.
- **A page when the rating drops or a streak breaks.** The number changes. The next page is "Back the next day" (24).
- **A share card.** Most people won't want this app's numbers leaving the phone.
- **"Don't lose your streak" warnings.** They push people with fear, and that is the wrong pressure for someone trying to quit.

## A first set

If you build only a few, these cover the moments that matter most:

1. **9, Morning check-in done.** Seen every day, so it does the most work.
2. **3, 7-day streak.** The page you asked for. 4 and 8 reuse it.
3. **18 and 19, Urge ridden out.** Rewards the hardest thing the app asks for.
4. **24, Back the next day.** The moment people are most likely to quit.
5. **28 and 29, Week complete and the laurel.** Gives each week an end and the 12 weeks a shape.
6. **37, Medallion earned.** The medallions exist but arrive without any moment.
7. **44 to 46, The weekly report tabs.**

## Decided

- **A slip resets the streak to 0.** "Best" stays.
- **The weekly report keeps its tabs.** Each tab plays when tapped.
- **The streak count is being fixed** to the rule above: the flame on Today and "Days in a row" in the Log.
- **The week strip on Today shows three icons** for past days: checked in, slip, and no check-in.
