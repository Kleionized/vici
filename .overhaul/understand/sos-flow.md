## 0. What changed, in one paragraph

The previous drop drew the SOS interrupt and the relapse boards as a **light paper sheet** (`#F4F3F0`, ink
`#131313` pills, warm blurred washes, object art made of `View`s), and the hub as a warm-gold night pane.
This drop redraws all of them in the flat dark kit: `#0D0D0D` ground + `noise.png` @ 0.05 for the interrupt
and relapse boards, `#111111` ground + `noise-dark.png` @ 0.09 for the hub; a kit `nav` row (back chevron ·
8 dashes or a 13px title · close ✕) at canvas top 60; Lato 700 headlines; `#F2F0EC` primary pills 58 tall;
every illustration is a flat 393×240 SVG "hero" on the kit's stage (horizon y 190, scaled about
`196px 190px`). Pickers lost their glyph grids (now chips, **multi-select**: "Select all that apply."), the
intensity read became a five-bar staircase, the three moves all close on "Continue" and two gain a body,
the completion board drops its count ("Logged as ridden out"), the hub is rebuilt pane by pane (no gold,
no wave chart, no stat rows) and gains a sixth board, **85F · Breathe**, that has no counterpart in the app.
