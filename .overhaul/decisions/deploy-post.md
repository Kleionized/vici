# Deploy: the owner's morning notes and letters (D550–D555)

Sources: `Vici_Morning_Notifications.docx` (84 morning notes, 25 for the morning after a slip) and
`Vici_Milestone_and_Relapse_Emails.docx` (25 milestone letters, 25 for the morning after a slip). The words are in
`src/content/morningNotes.ts` and `src/content/viciLetters.ts`, generated by `scripts/content/gen-post.py` (straight
apostrophes become ’; a quote and the line after it are split). `src/lib/viciPost.ts` chooses; nothing else writes copy.

## D550 — Each morning's notification carries that morning's note
The morning check-in reminder is now one dated notification per morning, four weeks ahead, on the ticked days at the
morning time, set again at every launch, return to the app, time change and new slip. Its body is the note for that
programme day (after day 84 the notes run again from day 1). The morning after a slip it is a relapse note instead: the
one about repeated slips (3 slip days in a week), tiredness or stress only when that was reported, else the next of the
22 general ones, so two slips in a row read different notes. `[user name]` is the first name, or is left out with the
comma around it. The night reminder is unchanged. The onboarding card previews Day 1's note.

## D551 — "Emails" are VICI's letters in the app
The app sends no email. The owner's letters arrive as VICI's post does: the envelope board over Today ("A letter
came."), the letter on its card, Save to Journal, and a row in Mail. Each is signed "Vici" as written. Sending them as
real email would need a mail provider, a sending domain and a server job, and is not done.

## D552 — When a letter arrives
The launch gate brings at most one arrival, now in this order: the letter for a slip, a milestone letter, the medallion
post, the week's report, the Week XII letter, the check-in.
- After a slip, the next morning: a slip logged today waits for tomorrow's first launch. The letter is chosen from the
  record: the first slip ever (R02), 3 slip days in a week (R06), what the slip was put down to (tired R07, stress or an
  argument R08, lonely R09, bored R10, sexual content or social R11, phone in bed, phone or late night R12, not sure
  R17), a clean run of a week or more ended (R03), else the next of R01, R22–R25. The letters for shame, frustration,
  giving up, withdrawing, fear, a difficult week, more support, harsh self-punishment, bargaining and a changed routine
  are kept but never chosen: the app does not ask about those, and they are not guessed.
- Milestones: a clean run is the days in a row without a slip, from the programme's first day, counted through
  yesterday (a night is whole before it is marked). A day the app was not opened counts as clean (the owner's rule for
  clean days; the streak, which needs a check-in, is a different number). M01–M18 at 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 14, 21, 30, 35,
  42, 50, 56, 60, 70, 84, 90, 100 days, M19–M22 at 4, 6, 9 and 12 calendar months, each once per account. M24 and M25
  (a night and a week back after a slip) once per run. When several are due only the furthest comes and the rest are
  spent. M23 comes once after the first urge ridden out, on a launch no run letter needs.
- Delivered letters and spent marks are kept per account on the phone (`tideline.letters.delivered.v1`), like the other
  delivery marks.

## D553 — The letter screen and Mail
`/letter?id=…&at=…` reads a delivered letter; without an id, the latest one, or "No letters yet." The old post-slip
letter ("Don't fail twice") and its Life Map quote are gone; Life Map's line no longer promises it. Mail lists every
delivered letter by its subject and date, "Saved to your journal" once kept.

## D554 — Existing accounts
An account already well into a clean run gets one letter on its first launch with this build, for the furthest mark it
has reached, and the earlier marks are spent.

## D555 — Not checked on a phone
The selection rules are covered by 45 cases in seven time zones, and both letter paths were run end to end in the web
preview. Notifications cannot run on the web: the dated morning schedule has not been seen firing on a device.
