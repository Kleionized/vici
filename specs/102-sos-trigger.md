## Reading — every row that is not `match`

The classes that differ without the screen differing, all recorded in
`DECISIONS.md`:

* **`radius: 50% → <n>px`** (D015) — React Native's `borderRadius` is a number.
  Every one is half the box's shorter side.
* **`radius: 50% → -` with a node count of 1 against 3** (D015) — a CSS
  `radial-gradient` div is `<Svg><Ellipse fill="url(#…)">`: three nodes on one
  rect. The canvas blurs several of these 4–5px; a closest-side radial already
  dies at its own edge, so the blur term is dropped (D010) rather than faked.
* **`bg: <colour> → -`** where the canvas's box became an SVG shape — the paint
  moves to `fill`, which the probe does not read as a background.
* **The canvas's painted `<span>` is the app's View plus Text** (D022).
* **`opacity: 0.07 → -`** on grain (D019).

A row marked **match (value)** is the account's rather than the canvas's.

Frame-specific: six full-width rows became a nine-card grid, and every label
changed. The ninth card sits alone in the last row and takes the full width,
which is what `flex: 1` on a single child does — the canvas draws it that way.

Two things the canvas does not draw and the app has to: **a selected card**
(none of the eighteen cards across the two grids is drawn selected, so the
treatment is `PickerRow`'s — a 1.6pt ink ring, an ink plate, a paper glyph and
the label a weight heavier), and **single-select** (the board this replaced was
multi-select with a check disc; nothing in the grid has one, and the branch
behind it is one board per answer).

Six of the nine glyphs belong to the labels of the list this grid replaced — a
heart on "Something online", a briefcase on "A stuck fantasy", a house on "Pure
habit", a banknote on "Can't sleep", a pulse line on "An argument". They are
drawn as the canvas draws them; see `DECISIONS.md` D036.

Every label and every glyph is read out of the frame by
`scripts/uifinal1/gen-pickers.mjs`, and `src/content/sosPickers.ts` is
byte-identical to a fresh read of the two frames.
