## Reading — every row that is not `match`

Three classes of row differ without the screen differing — `DECISIONS.md` D015,
D019 and D022 — plus the account's own values:

* **`radius: 50% → <n>px`.** React Native's `borderRadius` is a number, not a
  percentage; every one is exactly half the box's shorter side.
* **`radius: 50% → -` with a node count of 1 against 3.** A CSS
  `radial-gradient` div is drawn as `<Svg><Circle fill="url(#…)">` — a View, an
  Svg and a Circle on the same rect. The canvas also blurs these 4px; a
  closest-side radial already dies at its own edge, so the blur term is dropped
  (D010).
* **`radius: 50% 50% 0 0 / Npx … → -`.** An elliptical corner radius cannot be
  expressed in React Native, so each hill is an SVG arc drawn by
  `src/components/ui/Hill.tsx`. Same rect, same silhouette, three nodes.
* **`opacity: 0.07 → -`** on the grain: `expo-image` carries the opacity on the
  image and leaves its wrapper plain (D019).

A row marked **match (value)** is the account's rather than the canvas's: same
box, same colour, same type metrics, different characters. The frames are drawn
against a sample account on day 13 named Jerry; the seeded one is on day 41 and
named Marcus.

Frame-specific: the two 74pt answer discs the previous bundle drew are withdrawn
and replaced by a 2-up glyph row — one 54-tall row at `bottom: 84`, split down
the middle with a 12 gap, the left a white pill on a 1px ring holding a `#1D1C1A`
cross, the right an ink pill holding a white check. Neither `<path>` on the
canvas states a `fill`, so both fall back to black: the cross encloses nothing,
but the check's three points enclose a thin triangle that paints `#000000` over
the ink. `fill="none"` is stated on both here.

The card is pinned at the canvas's `top: 236` rather than centred in a band, and
its 248-tall night — four specks, the moon and its glow, the ridge, the bed, the
shelf, the phone and the `#E9D2A4` seal — matches value for value.

Four rows differ by decision rather than by accident, and `DECISIONS.md` D030
records why: the canvas marks the card with the **sun**, labels it **"Today"**
and fills it with `DAY_ACTIONS[0]`, under a title that asks whether the task was
completed. The app keeps the moon, "Last night" and yesterday's own action,
because what the screen does — ask about yesterday and write the answer to
yesterday's row — is the app's, and the canvas's reading makes the question
unanswerable. The card's height follows the copy, so its box and its foot report
as absent rather than as differing.
