## Resolutions

Same shape as `11 · Risky Times`, and cloned from it: this frame carries **`V3 Q5`'s glyphs
positionally**. Its "Relationships" tile draws the bed, "Sex or intimacy" the calendar,
"Self-control" the house and "Peace of mind" the phone. That is what the canvas draws and it is
what the app draws; it is noted here and in `src/content/onboardingFunnel.ts` rather than corrected.

Its hint reads "Choose up to three" where the other grids read "Select all that apply" — the copy
is the frame's own. The canvas draws no cap on the selection and the app enforces none.

The night field and progress rule are also `V3 Q5`'s — `DECISIONS.md` D014.
