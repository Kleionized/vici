Message choice (`assign`): the facts are computed from the entry and the moment (`facts`); a message fits when all its required facts hold. Keep the entry's message while it fits; else, among those that fit, drop the last 24 shown and the last two themes (relax in that order), rank by times shown, then last shown, then a specific theme before "general", then FNV-1a hash of `entryId + messageId`. One message in four (hash of `entryId:q` mod 4) ends on a quote: the first not yet seen that fits the theme (`*` any) and its facts.
