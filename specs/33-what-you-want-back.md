## Resolutions

**The bar chart is gone.** This drop draws a sunrise over two hills in a 240 × 200 art box at
76,176, and three 56pt white discs under it labelled `FOCUS`, `SLEEP` and `CONFIDENCE`, each with
a bespoke 22pt glyph. `19 · What it affects` offers nine answers and the bundle draws an icon for
none of the other six, so the trio is the board's own copy rather than a readout of what he
picked, and it is drawn as stated — `DECISIONS.md` D051.

**The ground shadow.** The canvas draws it as a **solid** `rgba(0,0,0,0.10)` ellipse, 208 × 14 at
16,152 inside the art box, under `filter: blur(5px)`. D010 absorbs a blur on a
`radial-gradient(closest-side, …)`, which has no edge to soften; this is the case D082 carves out,
where the blur *is* the shape. Pass 1 drew a radial falling from 0.10 at the centre to 0 at the
box edge, clipped to the box, which measured 14/255 too light at the shadow's top edge and left
the canvas's spill *below* the box unpainted. It is now the canvas's own solid under a real
`<FeGaussianBlur stdDeviation="5">` (`DECISIONS.md` D063), drawn in an `<Svg>` that overhangs the
box by 4σ so the viewport does not clip the falloff. Re-measured: 92,328 exact, 120,334 within
1/255, 260,334 exact.

The sun's own fill is `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 38%, …)`, whose implicit farthest-corner
radius on an 88pt square is 70.1 — 79.65 %, stated as `r`, `rx` and `ry` together per D064.

The corner bloom takes D105's fade stop. Whole-image: mean |Δ| 1.39/255 (1.53 before the two
fixes). The residue is ~120–160 device pixels per disc on the stroke edges of the three 22pt
glyphs, and the CTA pill's corner radius; the discs, their shadows and their icons all sit at the
canvas's own coordinates to the pixel.
