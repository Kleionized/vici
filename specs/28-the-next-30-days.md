## Resolutions

Thirty cells at `grid-template-columns: repeat(6,1fr); gap: 8` inside a 20pt-radius white card at
`padding: 16`, each cell `aspect-ratio: 1` at radius 10 — `#131313` for a day that ends with porn
and `#F7F6F3` under a `inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)` hairline otherwise. Which nine are inked
is read off the frame into `COST_30`, and the sentence above the card says "about 9" because
`NEXT_30_TIMES` counts that same array rather than restating the number. The card's height falls
out of the grid at the canvas's own 291.5.

The two rows still reported are the background washes (`DECISIONS.md` D015).

Whole-image: mean |Δ| 0.97/255 over the body, and the 871 device pixels over 6/255 inside the card
are all on the cells' 10pt corner radii — two rasterisers antialiasing the same rounded square.
