What research suggests
Research on substance use gives one reason to examine responses after setbacks. A trial involving 286 people compared usual aftercare with therapist-led relapse prevention and mindfulness-based relapse prevention after treatment.

Responsibility without punishment

Lesson 44 · 4 of 17

Lesson 44
Breines and Chen tested brief self-compassion exercises across four experiments. Some participants showed greater motivation to improve or make amends; in one experiment, they spent more time studying after a difficult test.

Lesson 44 · 7 of 17
9:41
