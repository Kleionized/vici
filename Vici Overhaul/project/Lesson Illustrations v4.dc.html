Rules applied across the set
One way to stand. Objects sit on the shared table, rooms on the shared floor line, outdoor scenes on the ground line. Symbols float over the same soft shadow.
Night only where it's night. No windows; stars are gone from batteries, thermometers and matches.
No merging. Where two white shapes touch, a thin ground-colour cut separates them.
The picture says the line. A non-linear chart for "Progress isn't linear", a lit first step for "the first part", cups facing each other for connection.
Drop-in compatible. Same stage and scale as before, so each SVG can replace the old one in place.
