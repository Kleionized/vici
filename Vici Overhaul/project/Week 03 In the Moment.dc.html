Part 1
What urge surfing is
Urge surfing means noticing an urge without immediately acting on it. The name can sound more complicated than the exercise. You’re making room for a feeling while keeping your hands off the search button.
Lesson 20 · 4 of 16
9:41
Lesson 20
Reported urges
No clear drop in the mindfulness group
Cigarettes over the next week
Fewer in the mindfulness group
In a study of 123 student smokers, Sarah Bowen and Alan Marlatt tested brief mindfulness instructions. Reported urges didn’t clearly fall more in the mindfulness group, but that group reported fewer cigarettes over the following week.
It was a short smoking study, not proof about this course. It does illustrate why feeling less craving and smoking less are different outcomes.
